## Overview

Use this page to pick a size for a part you build, so it lines up with Equality's own. Equality sizes text lines and controls on one scale of seven steps: 16, 20, 24, 28, 36, 44 and 52. Each step is the height of a line of text or of a box. The four [text styles](/getting-started/typography) are Tailwind text sizes, each on the step that is its line height. A text line follows its own size, and a row centres it among the controls beside it: a 24 section line sits in a 28 row with small buttons. Pick the step that fits what the part is. Give text lines and controls a step; icons have their own sizes.

Controls' size names are the step they sit on (`sm` 28, `md` 36). Avatar, Badge, Icon, Switch, Checkbox and Timeline use their own size names.

## When to Use Each Step

### 16

Small supporting text, such as a timestamp or a hint under a field. A small badge, only where space is tight.

### 20

Running text and descriptions, and anything that sits in a line of text, such as a default badge.

### 24

A card or section title's line. An extra-small control beside a field, such as the clear button inside a picker.

### 28

Small controls. A row holding a card or section title, centred, and the small buttons beside it, such as the header of `CardContentHeader`.

### 36

Default controls. A row holding the page title, 30 on a 36 line, and the default controls beside it. Regular table rows.

### 44

Large controls. Comfortable table rows, for tables people read slowly or use mostly by touch.

### 52

A picture or mark you build that should stand out more than a large control, such as a logo.

## Rows

A table row is 36, or 44 with `density="comfortable"`. Text that wraps makes the row taller.

## Building Your Own Component

Put a part you build yourself on a step, not a pixel value. The steps are options: the values Equality allows. The sizes chosen from them for controls and text are decisions. Read a decision where one fits:

- A control takes its height from `--eq-control-{xs,sm,md,lg}-height` (24, 28, 36, 44), such as `h-(--eq-control-md-height)`.
- Text takes one of the four styles on the [Typography](/getting-started/typography) page.
- A picture or mark that isn't a control, such as a logo or an icon in a circle, takes `--eq-size-{3xs,2xs,xs,sm,md,lg,xl}` (16 to 52), such as `size-(--eq-size-sm)`.
