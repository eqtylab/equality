## Overview

Equality sizes text lines, controls, rows and avatars on one scale of seven steps: 16, 20, 24, 28, 36, 44 and 52. Parts on the same step line up beside each other without extra spacing. Pick the step that fits what the part is. Give text lines, controls, rows and avatars a step; icons have their own sizes.

## When to Use Each Step

### 16

Small supporting text, such as a timestamp or a hint under a field. A small badge, only where space is tight.

### 20

Running text and descriptions, and anything that sits in a line of text: a default badge, or an avatar in a table, menu or list row.

### 24

An extra-small control beside a field, such as the clear button inside a picker. An `xs` avatar only beside an extra-small control.

### 28

A card or section title and the small button beside it. The one part a row holds: an avatar, a badge, a small switch or a default `IconButton`.

### 36

The page title and the default controls beside it. Regular table rows. An `md` avatar for one person on their own, such as a person's card or page header, or an "Assigned to" field.

### 44

Large controls. Comfortable table rows, for tables people read slowly or use mostly by touch. An `lg` avatar in a profile header.

### 52

The nav rail, and an app header that meets it at the corner. The `xl` avatar for a profile or page hero. On touch screens the rail grows, so an app header beside it should too.

## Rows

A table row is 36, or 44 with `density="comfortable"`. Either holds one part up to 28 without growing. Text that wraps, or a taller part, makes the row taller.

## Building Your Own Component

Put a part you build yourself on a step, not a pixel value:

- A control takes its height from `--eq-control-{xs,sm,md,lg}-height` (24, 28, 36, 44), such as `h-(--eq-control-md-height)`.
- Text takes one of the four styles on the [Typography](/getting-started/typography) page.
- A picture or mark that isn't a control, such as a logo or an icon in a circle, takes `--eq-size-{2xs,line,xs,sm,md,lg,xl}` (16 to 52), such as `size-(--eq-size-sm)`.
